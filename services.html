Questions clients ask

How long does it take to train a detection dog?

Basic detection imprinting takes 6 to 8 weeks. Full certification to NASDU standards takes 3 to 4 months, then the dog is recertified every year.

Do you supply guard dogs for homes?

Yes, for homes in Nairobi and across Kenya. Each dog comes with a handler session, so your family or guards know how to work with it safely.

Can you train my existing dog?

Only after a temperament and drive assessment: not every dog suits security or detection work. If yours does, we design a programme for it; if not, we say so and advise you.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Kitengela and work across Nairobi and Kenya, including Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret and Nakuru, and in Uganda and Tanzania.

Is dog walking or sitting available outside Nairobi?

Not yet. Walking and sitting are run by our own handlers in Nairobi only. Outside Nairobi, contact us and we will tell you what we can do.

How much does a trained guard dog cost?

It depends on the breed, the level of training and certification. A dog trained in obedience costs less than a NASDU-certified patrol or detection dog. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Is handler training open to individuals?

Yes. We train dog owners, security companies building a K9 unit, and government agencies, at individual and group rates. The NASDU pathway is open to every qualifying candidate.
