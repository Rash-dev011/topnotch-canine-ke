# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: security and operations leads at security firms, airports, sea ports, hotels, logistics companies and agencies in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania. They are buying detection or patrol capability for a site, or training for an in-house dog unit, and need to judge quickly whether this provider is credible, certified and able to deploy.

Secondary (a side line, not the lead): households and estates in Nairobi wanting a guard dog, dog walking or dog sitting. Also job seekers (K9 handlers, dog trainers, kennel technicians) reaching the careers section.

## Product Purpose

Top Notch Canine Capability trains and supplies working security dogs and their handlers. The site exists to turn a qualified institutional visitor into a "Request a K9 team" enquiry. Success is a request from a security firm, port, airport or hotel.

## Positioning

The head of training's own operational record: Bonifance Mokua, retired Kenya Defence Forces Military Police officer, certified IED and EDD trainer, who ran K9 security for cargo and aircraft screening at Aden Adde International Airport and Mogadishu sea port and led six UNMAS trainers mentoring the Somali Police Force. Teams are trained and certified to NASDU standards in Kitengela and recertified yearly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: detection dog training (cocaine, heroin, cannabis, methamphetamine, explosives); supply of trained dogs (patrol, VIP protection, detection, crowd deterrence); dog handler training; veterinary care; equipment and accessories; K9 consultation; dog walking and sitting (Nairobi households only).
- Ten working breeds: German Shepherd, Belgian Malinois (most deployed), Labrador Retriever, Springer Spaniel, Cocker Spaniel, Dutch Shepherd, Rottweiler, Belgian Shepherd, Russian Mountain Dog, South African Mastiff.
- Pages: home, services, breeds, about, contact (with careers), privacy, terms, 404.
- Stack: existing plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. The request form posts by mailto with a `data-endpoint` hook for a future form service.
- Open: no form backend yet.

## Product Principles

1. Credentials before claims: the trainer's record and NASDU certification carry the trust, not adjectives.
2. Institutions first: every page leads with what a security buyer needs to decide; household services stay visible but secondary.
3. Show the dogs at work: real operational photographs over generic imagery.
4. Never invent proof: no fabricated quotes, numbers or clients.
5. One clear action: "Request a K9 team" is reachable from every page.
