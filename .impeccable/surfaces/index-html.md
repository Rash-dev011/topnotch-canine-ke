## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a defence-grade security provider, executed at SafeLane's level: plain corporate authority, real field photographs, one red. It refuses tactical-costume styling (stencils, camo, glowing edges, mono labels).
OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink #111214, logo red as the only accent (#D4001C for filled controls), black and steel bands for weight. Jost (geometric, Futura-class) throughout. Square corners. Signature: hard diagonal cuts, on the hero's photo panel, the slanted end of every primary button, and the red/black split footer.
STORY: The visitor sees trained dogs at work in aircraft, buses and luggage halls, understands the services and who trains them (ex-Military Police, NASDU), sees real client names, and requests a team.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky white header (logo left, phone line and uppercase nav right, red slanted "Request a K9 team"; compacts on scroll). Below it, home: a full-bleed photograph (licensed stock, 2400px, uncaptioned atmosphere) with a translucent ink panel (0.86, light blur) on the left cut on a hard diagonal, holding the H1, one paragraph, the red slanted primary action and a secondary link; the credentials strip closes the band inside the first screen. Inner pages keep the ink-left / photo-right diagonal cut; client originals (~730px) show at ~46% width, near native size, and stock heroes at ~58%. Each closing band sits over a stock photograph that varies by page and never repeats the page hero.
