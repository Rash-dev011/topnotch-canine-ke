K9 teams for sites that cannot miss a threat

Top Notch Canine Capability trains, certifies and deploys working dogs and their handlers from our kennels in Kitengela.

Every dog is assessed for drive, temperament and health, trained to NASDU standards and certified before it is placed. Our clients are security firms, airports, sea ports, hotels, agencies and companies building a dog unit of their own.

  • Detection of cocaine, heroin, cannabis, methamphetamine and explosives
  • Patrol, VIP protection and crowd deterrence dogs
  • Handler courses for new handlers and refreshers
  • Veterinary care, equipment and K9 unit consultation

Trained to the standard of airport and sea-port K9 security in Mogadishu.

Our head of training ran K9 screening at Aden Adde International Airport and Mogadishu sea port, led six UNMAS trainers for the Somali Police Force, and served in the Kenya Defence Forces Military Police K9 unit.
