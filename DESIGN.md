# Design System: Top Notch Canine Capability

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Screening Line"**

The site reads like the inside of a well-run screening hall: white light, black ink, ruled lanes, and one red that means "act here". It is the category standard for a defence-grade security provider, plain corporate authority at the level of SafeLane Global. Atmosphere comes from large licensed photographs of working dogs; proof comes from the client's own field photographs of dogs working aircraft cabins, bus racks and luggage rows, shown close to their native size. Nothing is dressed up as tactical; the credentials do the work.

Density is calm and institutional. Sections breathe on a large vertical rhythm, content sits in ruled lists and two-column grids rather than floating cards, and the eye is led by weight bands: a white page punctuated by full-width ink and steel bands, with a warm stone band for secondary groupings. The home page opens on a full-bleed photograph under a translucent ink panel, and every page closes on an ink band laid over its own photograph. The one piece of geometry the system owns is the hard diagonal cut. It slices the hero panel and photographs, ends every primary button on a slant, trims photo panels, drives the photo reveal and splits the footer into red and black.

The system refuses tactical-costume styling: stencil type, camouflage, glowing edges, monospaced "classified" labels. It also refuses invented proof; stock imagery is atmosphere only and is never presented as the client's teams, so the record can speak.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White ground, ink type, one red for action.
- Jost 500 in sentence case for every heading, set large and tight; uppercase is reserved for small navigational labels.
- Square corners everywhere; the only non-rectangular shape is the diagonal cut.
- Ruled lists with a 2px ink top rule and 1px dividers stand in for cards.
- Full-width ink and steel bands carry weight; stone carries grouping.
- Licensed stock for atmosphere, client photographs for proof.
- One authored hero entrance and one narrow diagonal photo reveal; everything else is still.

## Colors

A near-monochrome corporate palette, white and ink with steel and graphite for depth, and a single saturated red reserved for action and large brand marks.

### Primary
- **Signal Red** (`signal-red`): the fill of every primary control ("Request a K9 team"), the active nav state, the header phone line, link hover, focus outlines on light ground, the input focus bar, error text, FAQ toggles, tick markers, list markers and prose links. Chosen over the logo red because white text on it passes 4.5:1.
- **Signal Red Deep** (`signal-red-deep`): hover and pressed state of red controls and the header phone link.
- **Logo Red** (`logo-red`): the logo's own red, used only for large marks on dark ground: the step numerals in the process band and the hover of the large phone number in the closing band. Never under small text, never as a button fill.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (`ink`): body text, headings, the hero band, the closing band, the black half of the footer, 2px top rules of every ruled list, the ink button and the badge border. At 86% opacity it is the home hero panel; at 80% to 94% it is the gradient that carries text over closing-band photographs.
- **Steel** (`steel`): the credentials strip that closes the hero band; one step lighter than ink so the strip reads as a shelf (92% opacity over the home photograph).
- **Graphite** (`graphite`): dividers inside dark bands, the ink button's hover, the phone action bar's gap line, scrollbar thumb.
- **Stone** (`stone`): the alternating light band behind grouped content (services, clients), table row hover, the white button's hover.
- **Rule** (`rule`): 1px dividers on light ground, the header's bottom border, the disabled button fill.
- **Muted** (`muted`): secondary text, ledes, descriptions in ruled rows, table cells, form hints.
- **Field Edge** (`field-edge`): the 1px border of every form field at rest; dark enough to hold 3:1 against white so the field reads as a control.
- **Placeholder** (`placeholder`): example text inside empty fields.
- **On-Dark** (`on-dark`) and **On-Dark Muted** (`on-dark-muted`): primary and secondary text on ink and steel.
- **White** (`white`): the page ground, the translucent header (88% with blur), headings on dark bands, text on red.

### Named Rules
**The One Red Rule.** Red appears only where the visitor can act or where the brand signs its name. It is never a background band, never a section tint, never decoration. The footer's red half is the one exception, because it is the brand mark at page scale.

**The Two Reds Rule.** Filled controls and small red text use Signal Red; the brighter Logo Red is for large marks on dark ground only. If the red element is smaller than 24px or carries white text, it is Signal Red.

**The Band Rule.** Weight comes from full-width bands (ink, steel, stone), never from tinted boxes inside the content column.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Jost (with Futura, Century Gothic, sans-serif), self-hosted variable woff2 (latin and latin-ext subsets), weights 100 to 900.
**Body Font:** Jost, same file.

**Character:** One geometric Futura-class family throughout. Medium weight at large sizes with tight leading and firm negative tracking gives headings an engineered, civic authority without shouting; regular weight at a generous 1.6 line height keeps body text plain and readable.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, `display`, 0.98, -0.03em): the H1 on inner pages and in prose pages. Balanced wrapping, sentence case.
- **Display home** (500, `display-home`, 0.98, -0.03em): the home hero H1, held smaller than Display so it fits the ink panel.
- **Headline** (500, `headline`, 1.04, -0.025em): section H2s, the closing band's statement, and the large phone number in the closing band (at 400, -0.01em).
- **Title** (500, `title`, 1.2, -0.01em): H3s, service names, row names in ruled lists, H2s inside legal prose.
- **Statement** (500, `statement`, 1.2, -0.015em): the record panel's statement, in sentence case.
- **Lede** (400, `lede`, 1.5): the paragraph under a heading and the hero paragraph, capped at about 34rem to 40rem, in Muted (or On-Dark Muted on ink). Trio item heads and FAQ summaries use this size at 500.
- **Body** (400, `body`, 1.6): running text, with a 68ch measure for long paragraphs and 72ch for legal prose.
- **Label** (500, `label`, uppercase, 0.06em to 0.08em): main navigation, "more" links (at 0.875rem), the phone menu toggle, table column heads and contact list labels (0.8125rem, 0.08em), footer column heads (0.8125rem, 0.12em), certification badges.
- **Step numeral** (300, 3rem, tabular): the numbered steps of the deployment sequence, in Logo Red.

### Named Rules
**The Sentence Case Rule.** Every heading, button and statement is set in sentence case at weight 500. Uppercase is reserved for short navigational and tabular labels of a few words; it is never used for headings, statements or paragraphs.

**The One Family Rule.** Jost only. No second display face, no monospace, no stencil.

**The Words Not Gauges Rule.** Ratings such as a breed's drive are written as words in Ink ("High", "Extreme", "Medium to high"), never as bars, dots or meters.

## Layout

A centred column of max 1280px with a fluid gutter (`gutter`, 20px to 80px) on each side. Sections stack on a fluid vertical rhythm (`section`, 88px to 168px), with a tight variant at 60% of it. Content inside a section is arranged in two-column grids with unequal fractions (1.35 : 1 for intro and record, 1.2 : 1 for band text and contact, 0.8 : 1.2 for founder portrait and body, 1.15 : 1 for the field photographs, 1.4 : 1 for form and contact list) and column gaps of 40px to 96px. Section heads pair the H2 and a lede side by side, aligned to the end, collapsing to a stack under 800px.

Ruled lists run on fixed column counts: four credentials, three services, five client names, four steps, three trio items, each separated by 1px vertical rules rather than gaps. They step down to two columns and then one at 900px and 560px.

The header is sticky and translucent white: 104px tall at the top of the page, compacting to 76px once the page has scrolled 24px (the logo shrinks from 58px to 46px and the phone line above the nav is dropped on desktop).

The hero is a full-width ink band in three variants:
- **Home (full):** the photograph runs edge to edge; the H1, paragraph and actions sit in a translucent ink panel on the left, max 680px wide, that extends to the viewport edge and ends on a 72px diagonal (`slant-panel`). The band is clamp(600px, 100svh minus header and credentials, 860px) tall so the credentials strip closes it inside the first screen.
- **Inner page:** the photograph occupies the right 46% (client originals, kept near native size) and the text sits on solid ink, min(60vh, 540px) tall.
- **Inner page, stock:** the same with the photograph at 58%, for licensed stock.

Under 1024px the photograph moves above the text at full width, cut along its foot, and the home panel's ink plate is dropped.

The closing band on content pages is laid over a per-page photograph: text in a single column of max 36rem on the left, the phone number below a translucent white rule, padding of `section-band-photo` (104px to 200px). On desktop the image is widened to 125% and pushed right so the subject sits clear of the text.

Breakpoints: 1024px (header collapses to a menu toggle; hero photograph stacks; band photo goes to a flat 80% ink scrim), 900px (two-column grids collapse), 800px (section heads and ruled rows stack), 768px (phone action bar appears), 600px (form becomes one column), 560px (steps go to one column), 420px (header tightens).

On phones a fixed two-button action bar (call in ink, request in red) slides up once the hero's primary button has scrolled out of view and hides again over the footer.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat at content level. Depth comes from tonal bands (white, stone, steel, ink), 1px rules, photographs and translucency, not shadows on surfaces. Two pieces of chrome cast soft, diffuse shadows because they float over the page: the sticky header once the page scrolls, and the open phone menu. Translucent ink carries text over photographs: the home hero panel (86% ink with a 6px backdrop blur), the closing band's left-weighted ink gradient, the inner hero photo's left-edge gradient, and field captions on 88% ink. The header itself is 88% white with a 14px backdrop blur.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Header lift** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -18px rgb(17 18 20 / 0.45)`): the sticky header once the page has scrolled.
- **Menu drop** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 40px -24px rgb(17 18 20 / 0.35)`): the open phone navigation panel only.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Ground Rule.** Surfaces, buttons, lists and photos never cast shadows. If something needs to feel raised, put it on a different band. Only floating chrome (the sticky header and the phone menu) may cast a soft shadow, and never a hard offset one.

**The Readable Photo Rule.** Text never sits directly on a photograph; it sits on ink, either solid or as a translucent plate or gradient of at least 80% where the text is.

## Shapes

Every corner is square (`rounded.none`), including inputs and buttons. The one recurring silhouette is the hard diagonal cut, made with clip-path polygons, never with rotation or skewed boxes:

- **Home hero panel:** the translucent ink plate ends on a 72px diagonal at its right edge.
- **Inner hero photograph:** its left edge is cut on a diagonal from 22% at the top to 0 at the bottom; on phones the cut moves to the bottom edge, rising 40px.
- **Primary buttons:** square start, slanted end; the right edge loses `slant` (18px, or `slant-sm` 14px on small buttons) at the bottom.
- **Photo panels:** service photos lose 12% of height on a foot diagonal; split and founder photos lose 8% at one bottom corner; the record photo is cut top and bottom; field captions end on a 24px slant.
- **Photo reveal:** the reveal opens on the same leaning diagonal, a wedge that sweeps across the frame.
- **Tick markers:** a 12px red block with a slanted end, echoing the button.
- **Footer:** a red field covers the left of the ink footer, cut on a diagonal from 72% at the top to 58% at the bottom; on phones the split becomes a near-horizontal diagonal.

Borders are 1px Rule on light ground, 1px Field Edge on form fields and 1px Graphite on dark; list tops are 2px Ink (2px White in the process band).

### Named Rules
**The Cut Rule.** Steep cuts (hero panel and photo, button ends, footer split, caption ends, the reveal wedge) always lean right as they rise; shallow cuts across a photo's top or foot may face either way to point toward the adjacent text. Every cut is a hard edge. No curves, no rounded corners, no chamfers on all four corners.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident, flat and directional: a square start and a slanted end, like a lane marker.
- **Shape:** square corners with the right edge slanted by 18px (`slant`); `slant-sm` 14px on the header button.
- **Primary:** Signal Red fill, white Jost 500 at body size, 52px min height, padding 14px 42px 14px 24px (extra right padding absorbs the slant). A 14px arrow follows the label.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill shifts to Signal Red Deep over 180ms and the arrow nudges 4px right over 220ms; focus shows a 2px outline offset 3px (Signal Red on light ground, white on dark).
- **Ink and White:** the same shape in Ink (hover Graphite) and White (hover Stone) for dark and light contexts.
- **Small:** 44px min height, 0.9375rem, used in the header.
- **Disabled:** Rule fill with Muted text.
- **Secondary action:** a plain underlined text link beside the button, not a second button.

### Links
- **Inline link:** underline at 1px, offset 0.25em, in 40% of the text colour; on hover the underline and text turn Signal Red (white on dark).
- **"More" link:** uppercase label in Signal Red (white on dark) with a 14px arrow that nudges 4px on hover.

### Ruled lists (in place of cards)
The system has no cards. Grouped content is set as ruled lists: a 2px Ink top rule, items divided by 1px Rule lines, generous padding (22px to 28px), and no backgrounds. Variants: credentials strip (four columns on steel), client names (five columns), trio (three columns), steps (four numbered columns on ink), rows (name, description and meta per line, used for services and breeds), timeline (date and event), contact list (uppercase label over value), FAQ (details elements with a red plus that turns 45 degrees when open), and the breeds table (uppercase column heads, stone row hover, the drive column in Ink words).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white field, 1px Field Edge border, square corners, 52px min height, 12px 14px padding, Jost 400 at 1rem, Placeholder text; labels sit above in Jost 500 at 0.9375rem, optional markers in Muted.
- **Hover / Focus:** border goes to Ink; on focus a 3px Signal Red bar appears along the bottom edge (inset shadow) and the caret is red.
- **Error:** the red border and bottom bar plus a 0.875rem red message under the field. Selects use a drawn chevron.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** sticky, translucent white header (88% with blur), 104px tall, logo and two-line wordmark left; on the right, the phone line in Signal Red above uppercase nav links (0.9375rem, 500, 0.06em) and the small red button. Once scrolled it compacts to 76px over 300ms, drops the phone line and lifts on the header shadow. Hover draws a 2px red underline from the left over 240ms; the current page is red with the underline held.
- **Phone (under 1024px):** 80px header, phone icon in a 44px ruled square, a bordered "Menu" toggle that swaps to "Close"; the menu opens as a full-width white panel of ruled rows below the header with the red button at the end. Escape closes it.

### Hero band (signature)
Full-width ink band with the photograph and the diagonal cut, the H1 and paragraph, the red primary action plus a text link, and a steel credentials strip closing the band. The home variant lays a translucent ink panel cut on a 72px diagonal over a full-bleed licensed photograph; inner pages keep the ink-left, photo-right cut.

Entrance, played once on load: on the home page the photograph fades in (1600ms) while settling from scale 1.06 (2200ms) and the ink panel wipes in along its diagonal (1000ms); on inner pages the photograph wipes in along its diagonal (1100ms). On every page the H1, paragraph and actions rise 14px and fade in at 220, 300 and 380ms. All use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; on phones the photo fades instead of wiping. This is the system's only entrance, and it is disabled under reduced motion.

### Photo reveal
A narrow, rationed scroll moment: a photograph develops on a diagonal wedge (clip-path, 1100ms) while settling from scale 1.08 (1600ms), the first time it enters the screen. It is applied only to the three home service photographs (staggered 0, 90 and 180ms) and to the first split photograph on each page. Anything already on screen when the page loads shows at once. Reduced motion and print disable it.

### Record panel
A ruled white panel beside the intro: a sentence-case statement at the Statement size, a supporting paragraph, the founder's photograph cut top and bottom, and a footer line with the name, role, link and a small logo.

### Closing band over a photograph
The last band on content pages: an ink band laid over its own photograph, with a left-weighted ink gradient (94% to 80% across the text side, 25% to 45% over the image) so the statement, lede, actions and large phone number read on the left. Under 1024px the scrim flattens to 80% ink.

### Footer (signature)
Ink footer with a Signal Red field split off on a diagonal; three link columns under small uppercase heads on the red side, the logo, phone and address right-aligned on the ink side, and a legal line under a 1px translucent white rule that carries the photograph credit for licensed stock.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Signal Red (#d4001c) for every filled control and Logo Red (#fe0022) only for large marks on ink.
- **Do** set every heading in Jost 500, sentence case, with tight leading and negative tracking (0.98 and -0.03em on H1, 1.04 and -0.025em on H2).
- **Do** end primary buttons on an 18px slant and keep all corners square.
- **Do** group content as ruled lists with a 2px Ink top rule and 1px dividers.
- **Do** give weight with full-width ink, steel or stone bands.
- **Do** use the client's own photographs as proof, near their native size, and licensed stock only for atmosphere, credited in the footer.
- **Do** put text over photographs only on ink plates or gradients of at least 80%.
- **Do** keep motion to the one hero entrance, the rationed photo reveal and short 160ms to 300ms state transitions, and honour reduced motion and print.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use stencil type, camouflage, glowing edges or monospaced labels.
- **Don't** add rounded corners, pill buttons or shadows on buttons, photos or panels.
- **Don't** put content in cards with backgrounds and shadows; use rules.
- **Don't** use red as a background band, section tint or decoration outside the footer split.
- **Don't** set headings, statements or paragraphs in uppercase.
- **Don't** caption or present stock photographs as the client's dogs, handlers or sites.
- **Don't** extend the photo reveal beyond the home services trio and the first split photograph per page, and don't add a second entrance animation.
- **Don't** show ratings as bars, dots or meters; write them as words.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface.
