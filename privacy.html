Privacy policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Last updated November 2026.

1. Who we are

Top Notch Canine Capability ("we", "us", "our") is a K9 security and dog training company registered in Kenya, operating from Balozi Road, Bishop Court, Kitengela. We are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us and to processing it in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact form, by calling us, or by emailing or messaging us directly. This may include:

  • Your name and the name of your company or organisation
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your site location or address relevant to the service you are enquiring about
  • Details of your security requirements as provided in your message

We do not set cookies, tracking pixels or analytics scripts on this website. Our typefaces are served from this website, so loading a page does not contact a third-party font service. If you use our contact form, it opens your own email app with the details filled in, and you send them to clientservice@topnotchk9capability.co.ke from there. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide the services you have requested
  • Prepare quotations and consultation plans
  • Communicate with you about ongoing deployments or service arrangements

We do not use your personal information for any marketing, profiling or automated decision-making purposes.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your details internally with members of our team who need them to respond to your enquiry or deliver services. We use a small number of service providers to operate this website and receive enquiries (our email provider, and WhatsApp/Meta for chat); they process your data only to deliver that service. Some of these providers may store data outside Kenya, in which case we rely on the safeguards permitted under the Data Protection Act, 2019. Otherwise, we will only share information with external parties if required to do so by Kenyan law.

5. How we store and protect your data

Enquiry data received by email or WhatsApp is stored on our secure business devices and email systems. We take reasonable steps to ensure this information is protected against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. Data is retained only for as long as it is relevant to your ongoing or potential service relationship with us.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain it
  • Object to, or withdraw consent for, the processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at clientservice@topnotchk9capability.co.ke.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your request, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya — www.odpc.go.ke.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page. Continued use of our website or services after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
